The classic defaults are the look tuned on the home stage against the system's control center: `frost` 0.54 (was 0.35), `specular` 0.85 (was 0.7), and a surface's base `blur` 2px (was 1). `defaultGlassTheme` carries the first two; a theme or a surface that sets them itself is unaffected. `prismGlassTheme`'s `blurScale` is restated against the new base and draws the same blur as before.
