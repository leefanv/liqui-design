Add the `prism` finish and the lighting dials. `finish` picks the light model: `classic` is the shipped look, lit along the top and bottom edges and frosted; `prism` is one key light from the top left, a dark far rim, a hairline round the outline and a clear, brightened interior — refracting glass type, as a surface. `lightAngle`, `edgeShade`, `hairline`, `brightness` and `tintFloor` can be set on the theme or on a surface and otherwise follow the finish, so switching the finish alone switches the whole look. `prismGlassTheme` is the prism finish with the optics it is drawn for. Classic output is unchanged.
